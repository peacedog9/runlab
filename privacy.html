Run Lab

Privacy policy

Last updated 19 August 2026

The short version

Run Lab collects nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking, and no network connections of any kind. Your workout history and settings never leave your device.

What is stored

Your chosen settings (lane, cue mode, console lead, difficulty) and your completed workout history are saved in your browser's local storage (web version) or the app's private storage (installed version). This data stays on the device and is never transmitted anywhere.

Your control

  • Export: Guide tab, Export. Your entire history as plain JSON, yours to copy anywhere.
  • Delete everything: Guide tab, Import lets you replace state, or simply remove the home-screen app / clear site data. Uninstalling removes every byte.

Third parties

None. The app makes zero network requests: no servers, no SDKs, no crash reporting, no advertising identifiers.

Permissions

The installed app requests no permissions. Speech and audio cues run entirely on-device.

Health disclaimer

Run Lab is a training tool, not medical advice. Stop and seek help for chest pain, faintness, or unusual shortness of breath.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on the project's public repository.
